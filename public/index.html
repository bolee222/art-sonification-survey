Ponify: Pose-based Art Sonification Method to Convey Sense of Dynamics of the Paintings

Hello!
This website is a demo page for our Ponify Project. Ponify is an art sonification method that generates music that expresses ‘the sense of dynamics’ of a given painting. To do so, we anlayze the ‘poses of people in the paintings’, and applied it to musical parameters.
