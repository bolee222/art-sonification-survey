#### Ponify: Pose-based Art Sonification Method to Convey Sense of Dynamics of the Paintings



### Hello! This website is a demo page for our **Ponify Project**. Ponify is an art sonification method that generates music that expresses '_the sense of dynamics_' of a given painting. To do so, we anlayze the '_poses of people in the paintings_', and applied it to musical parameters.
